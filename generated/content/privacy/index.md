## How this website handles information

Cybernetks is a studio operated by Agilix BV in Belgium. This page explains what information may be processed when you visit this website or contact Cybernetks.

## Information processed when you visit

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. Cybernetks does not collect information through accounts, forms, comments, or a newsletter.

GitHub may log technical information, including your IP address, to deliver and secure GitHub Pages. This processing is handled by GitHub. You can read more in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## When you contact Cybernetks

If you email Cybernetks through a project support address, Cybernetks receives the information you choose to provide. This can include your name, email address, message, device or app details, and any attachments you send.

That information is used to reply to your message, investigate a problem, and maintain the relevant project. Please remove private information from screenshots or recordings when it is not needed.

Messages and attachments are kept only for as long as they are needed for the conversation, support history, legal obligations, or the protection of legitimate interests.

## Cookies and analytics

Cybernetks does not currently use cookies or analytics on this website. There is therefore no cookie banner. This page will be updated before analytics or other tracking technology is introduced.

## External websites

This website links to services such as GitHub, YouTube, Spotify, Twitch, Itch.io, LinkedIn, Threads, and Buy Me a Coffee. Those services operate their own websites and privacy practices. Their policies apply after you follow a link to them.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to data portability and to complain to the [Belgian Data Protection Authority](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be/).

For website privacy questions or requests, email [info@cybernetks.be](mailto:info@cybernetks.be). For information sent to an individual Cybernetks project, you can also use the contact address on that project's support page.
