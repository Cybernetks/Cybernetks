This page is inspired by [uses.tech](https://uses.tech) which is a directory (made by [@wesbos](https://twitter.com/wesbos)) of tech people sharing specifics about the hardware/software they use.

## Development

[Jetbrains PhpStorm](https://www.jetbrains.com/phpstorm/)—My main editor to work with for client and personal web development projects

[Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org)—The engine I use for game development. They have their own IDE that I also use. Personally, I installed it through Steam to keep track of the hours I work in it as a fun stat.

[Warp](https://www.warp.dev)—I recently switched terminals from iTerm2 to this one.

[Fork](https://git-fork.com)—I'm still looking for a great visual git client but I always tend to default back to the terminal for git.

[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md)—My go to tool for notes and simple projects management. I used to roll with Apple Notes itself but I like some of the extras Obsidian provides.

[Aseprite](https://www.aseprite.org)—Game development also means art assets. While learning pixel art you'll find me struggling in Aseprite.

[Blender](https://www.blender.org)—The go to tool for 3D assets during game development. I might also do a little modelling for non game related fun.

[FamiStudio](https://famistudio.org)—Another aspect of game development is sound. For this I'll mostly be experimenting in FamiStudio for that NES feeling. I might other music editors later.

[FireFox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/new/)—I mostly make use of FireFox for development as I'm so used to it for the developer tools. For personal browsing I tend to stick to Apple Safari.

## Hardware

[16-Inch Macbook Pro M1 Max 64GB](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/)—My development machine of choice. Not ideal for gaming itself but development goes fine on this one.

[Apple Studio Display](https://www.apple.com/studio-display/)—I splurged a bit and got myself 2 Apple Studio Displays to work from. I usually have 1 in landscape and 1 in portrait mode for maximum efficiency.

[Secretlab Magnus Pro](https://secretlab.eu/pages/magnus-pro)—A great standing desk with lots of space. I went with the black version. I also added the [dual monitor arm](https://secretlab.eu/products/magnus-monitor-arm?sku=MAG-MONARM2-BLK), a [single monitor arm](https://secretlab.eu/products/magnus-monitor-arm?sku=MAG-MONARM1-BLK) and the [laptop mount](https://secretlab.eu/products/laptop-mount-attachment?sku=M-LAPTOPMNT-BLK).

2[Secretlab Titan Evo](https://secretlab.eu/pages/titan-evo-2022-series)—Chair of choice while keeping in theme with the rest of the setup.

[Elgato Wave3](https://www.elgato.com/ww/en/p/wave-3-black)—My microphone for meetings and streaming. Though I sometimes also switch to the next item in the list.

[Apple Airpods Pro](https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/)—My go to headset for when I'm on the go. Wether that is at a client's office or a personal retreat.

[Logitech MX Master](https://www.logitech.com/en-gb/discover/c/master-series)—After trying some of the Apple products I decided to go with the Logitech MX Master collection for keyboard and mouse.
