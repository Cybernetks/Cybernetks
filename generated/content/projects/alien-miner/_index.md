Alien Miner is my entry for Brackey’s Game Jam 2025.2, built in Godot. It is a platformer with a mining twist: collect gems, risk it all on dangerous paths, and bank your haul before it is lost.

The jam theme was “Risk it for the biscuit.” In the game, you need to bank enough gems to reach the end gate. Dying before you bank them means losing what you collected.

The jam build has one level. It was exported, uploaded to Itch.io, and submitted at the end of the week. This was my first game jam, and the development Logs document the journey from the initial idea to the final submission.

Read the [game jam summary](/brackeys-game-jam-2025-2-summary/) or [view Alien Miner on Itch.io](https://cybernetks.itch.io/alien-miner).
