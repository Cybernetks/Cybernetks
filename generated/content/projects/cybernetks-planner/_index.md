The Cybernetks Planner is a lightweight planning system built for developers, creators, builders, and anyone working toward long-term goals.

It starts with a simple idea: planning should create action, not become the work itself.

Version 0.1 contains two PDF pages: a Yearly Overview and a Monthly Overview. They can be printed or imported into a note-taking app on a reMarkable, iPad, or Android tablet.

The yearly page starts with a single focus and breaks it into monthly milestones. The monthly page turns one milestone into weekly actions. Both include a small log or debrief for reflecting on what worked and what to carry forward.

This is a first iteration, with a deliberately simple design. Read [A First Release](/003-a-first-release/) for the thinking behind it.
