Cybernetks is my one‑person studio — a space where creative software, experiments, and real-world tools come together.

By day, I help businesses build modern, scalable web applications using Symfony, Laravel, and React. By night (or whenever life allows), I explore indie game development, creative tools, and experiments that fuel my curiosity.

This site is where I document what I’m building, what I’m learning, and what I’m figuring out as a solo creator — one log at a time.

Whether you’re a developer, a potential client, a fellow builder, or just someone curious about the process: welcome to [The Grid](https://discord.gg/KfbErDfkud).

## What I do

- Freelance web development (Symfony, Laravel, React, Vue)
- API development and scalable backend systems
- Creative tools, UI/UX systems, and digital experiments
- Indie game development in Godot
- Building in public — through logs, streams, and transparency

## Why I built Cybernetks

I’ve always juggled creative and technical pursuits — client work, game design, experimental tools, art, and even photography. Cybernetks brings it all under one roof. No more scattered identities — just one focused system for the things I love to build and share.

This isn’t a company in the traditional sense. It’s a studio. A creative lab. A solo product space. A place to explore ideas I care about — and hopefully make things that help, entertain, or inspire along the way.
