I'm Kenneth Schabrechts, the person behind Cybernetks. I build games and tools that I want to see in the world, from the first idea through to something people can use or play.

I spend a lot of my time building software for other people. Cybernetks gives me room to work on my own ideas and learn what it takes to release them. Right now that includes [Operator](/projects/operator/), an iOS app for focusing on today, a [printable planner](/projects/cybernetks-planner/), and [Alien Miner](/projects/alien-miner/), a game I made for a jam.

I write about the work as it happens. The [Logs](/logs/) cover progress, decisions, and things I learn along the way. The [Monthly Snapshots](/snapshots/) give me a chance to look back at what moved forward and what didn't. Some updates are small. That's fine. They don't need to become a video or podcast before I can share them here.

Cybernetks is one of the ventures I run through Agilix BV.

You can also find Cybernetks on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cybernetks/), [GitHub](https://github.com/Cybernetks), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Cybernetks).
