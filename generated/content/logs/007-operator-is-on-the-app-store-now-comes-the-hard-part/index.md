Operator is on the App Store.

I can finally write that. My first iOS app is available for iPhone and iPad, and you can download it, try it, and tell me what you think.

That last part is important, because getting the app released means I can finally move beyond my own ideas about what it needs. Now I need to find out how it feels to other people.

But first, there was a fair amount of waiting.

## Three weeks of wondering

After uploading Operator to App Store Connect, I heard back the very next day. There were some questions to answer, and I needed to record a video showing how the app worked and what it looked like.

I sent everything back. Then I heard nothing for three weeks.

Long enough to start wondering whether I had done something wrong. Had I actually submitted the response? Was there another step I had missed? Was the application just sitting somewhere waiting for me?

It had been submitted. I was waiting.

I cannot tell you what anyone else's first review will look like. Mine took longer than I expected, and the lack of feedback made it difficult to know what was happening.

On 4 November, the good news finally arrived: Operator had been reviewed and approved. I released it immediately.

After all the preparation, the app was out in the wild.

## What Operator does

Operator is a productivity app for builders and makers. The current version lets you organise projects, milestones, and tasks, then choose what you want to focus on.

The focus selector is the part I want to explain properly, because a list of features does not really tell you why the app exists.

You can select a project, narrow things down to a milestone, or work with your standalone tasks. The home screen changes to show the tasks belonging to that focus. The other tasks stay out of the way while you work on what you selected.

That is the basic idea: keep the structure for the things you are building, then give yourself a smaller view when it is time to do the work.

There is still plenty I want to improve. This is the first version, and I am not pretending every detail is finished. But it is available now, which means I can start hearing from people who use it differently from me.

## Tell me what feels right, and what does not

If you try Operator, I would like to hear what you like, what bothers you, and what feels missing.

Something can seem clear while I am building it and feel awkward to someone opening it for the first time. Equally, something I barely think about might turn out to be the part someone enjoys most.

Both kinds of feedback help. I want to know what to improve, but also what is already working well enough to keep.

You can find [Operator - Daily Focus on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/operator-daily-focus/id6819861949).

The Founding Supporter Pass is also still available for people who would like to support the app early. At the time of recording, it was €9.99 in my local storefront, with prices varying by region. It includes an exclusive icon, a little heart in the app title, and lifetime access to future paid subscription features.

Those future features still need to be built. For now, the immediate work is getting feedback on the app that is already there and using that to help decide what comes next.

## Now I need to get it into people's hands

I think the next part will be more difficult for me: marketing.

I have never really done it. Building gives me something concrete to work on. Getting the app in front of people means putting myself out there, trying things, and seeing whether any of them work.

Making these Logs feels related. I am sharing work while I am still figuring it out, without knowing exactly what will connect with someone.

I do not have a neat marketing lesson to share yet. This is the point where I start trying.

## A simpler way to keep sharing

I have also been working on the recording and streaming setup. There has been a bit of overthinking involved. Of course there has.

The lighting still needs work, but I am getting closer to a setup I enjoy using. I want these Logs to become a little shorter and a little more frequent. I know I have said that before. I am trying.

There is a game project I want to start when the streams begin, too. That can wait for another Log. This one is about finally getting Operator released and finding out what happens when people can use it.

If you are building something and need a way to narrow your attention to the work in front of you, give Operator a try. Then tell me how it feels and what would make it work better for you.
