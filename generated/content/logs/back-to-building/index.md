It took a lot longer than I thought, but I’m finally here—with a clear mind, a focused brand, and both hands back on the keyboard.

**Cybernetks is no longer “just” a game dev studio.**
It’s now my unified identity as a solo creator:

- I build web apps and tools for clients (mostly Symfony, Laravel, React)
- I design indie games in Godot, because I love it
- I share the process, experiments, and dev thoughts right here

After months of juggling too many ideas, side accounts, and creative false starts, I shut everything down and merged it into one studio.
That’s what this blog—and Cybernetks—is all about now.

I’ll be sharing progress logs (from client work and games), lessons learned, tools I’m playing with, and small updates as I build things out.
Some posts will be technical, some creative, some messy—but all of it will be real.

I’m not chasing algorithms, going viral, or launching a product tomorrow.
I’m just showing up again—one line of code, one pixel, one post at a time.

- Starting a custom finance dashboard using Symfony—to track both personal and professional income, expenses, and subscriptions
- Making my learning visible instead of hidden

The fun begins now.
And this time, I’m building in public.
