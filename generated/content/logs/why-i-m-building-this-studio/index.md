I've had the idea for a studio floating in my head for a while now. I started the branding process, second-guessed it, polished it a bit more, and pushed it aside more times than I can count.

But a few weeks ago, after a talk with a colleague, something clicked. I missed ownership, on multiple fronts. So I took it, I took ownership of my idea, sat down and finished the core identity of what Cybernetks stands for—mission, vision, values, tone, and even the visual branding.

Now, I have a clear mission that I want to achieve with Cybernetks:
**To create immersive and innovative indie games & web applications that inspire, entertain or solve real problems**

I also have a clear vision:
**To be recognised as a helpful, community-driven indie game & web developer—known for innovation, storytelling, honest learning, and sharing it openly**

The decision to do it all in public is both exciting and scary. Especially for someone like me, who might care a little too much about what others think of me.

The tagline is simple and says exactly what I intent to do:
**Code. Create. Play.**

This is my starting line. Let's see where it goes.
