The last two days were focused on finishing touches and getting **Alien Miner** out the door. It’s officially exported, uploaded, and submitted to the jam!

## **What I built**

- **End Gate**- Added a simple congratulations message since there’s only one level in the jam build.
- **Guidance**- Added labels near the start of the game to help players understand the basics.
- **Export & Submission**- Exported the build and uploaded it to Itch.io: [Alien Miner](https://cybernetks.itch.io/alien-miner)
- Submitted to the Brackey’s Game Jam: [Jam Entry](https://itch.io/jam/brackeys-14/rate/3851538)

## **Notes**

- I’ve been feeling a bit off, and with family taking priority over the weekend, progress wasn’t as fast as I’d hoped.
- I’m still unsure if the “risky path” really sells the theme strongly enough — finding the right balance there is something I clearly need more practice with.

## **Next Steps**

- Write a blog post in the coming days reflecting on my first game jam experience.
- Watch how **Alien Miner** is received during the jam. If feedback is positive, I may turn this into a proper project for the year:- Expanded storyline
- More mechanics
- More levels
- Integrated or custom assets to bring it all together
- Potential full release on Itch.io or even Steam

## **Reflections**

Even though the final build is small, it’s complete and I shipped it. That’s the real win of this jam. I now have a published game, a working loop, and a ton of lessons to carry forward into future projects.

Don't forget to have a look at the game: [Alien Miner](https://cybernetks.itch.io/alien-miner) and let me know what you think!

Catch you in the next log.
